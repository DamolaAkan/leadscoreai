## PAGE 1 — The opportunity

### The market moved, and financing did not follow

The removal of the fuel subsidy and the persistent instability of the national grid have changed backup power from a convenience into a household and business necessity. Across Lagos, Abuja and every commercial corridor in between, families and small businesses are replacing generators with inverter-and-battery systems that cost between ₦400,000 and ₦4,000,000.

Almost all of these purchases happen in **cash.** Distributors sell what a customer can pay for today, not what the customer actually needs — because there is no financing layer sitting between the shelf and the buyer. The result is a large, growing, credit-hungry market that no lender has cleanly captured.

### Why this is a bank opportunity, not a distributor one

An inverter distributor has product and demand but no balance sheet and no credit discipline. A customer has need but not the full amount in cash. **[BANK NAME] has the one thing the whole chain is missing — capital — and the credibility to lend it.**

The single reason banks have hesitated on consumer-asset lending of this kind is default risk: how do you know, at the point of sale, which buyer will keep paying for eighteen months and which will stop after three? That question — *willingness and ability to repay* — is precisely the problem LeadScoreAI was built to solve.

## PAGE 2 — How the model works

### The flow, end to end

1. **Demand.** LeadScoreAI runs targeted digital campaigns and a branded online scorecard that a prospective buyer completes in about two minutes. It captures need, budget, location, income signals and — critically — behavioural signals of repayment intent.

2. **Qualification and scoring.** Every applicant receives a **Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) score** on a uniform 1–100 scale, the way a credit score works. This is not a self-reported form. It is a model that weights the answers most correlated with real repayment, and it improves every month as actual outcomes flow back in.

3. **Hand-off to the bank.** Only applicants who clear the agreed threshold are passed to [BANK NAME] for final credit approval. The bank sees a clean, pre-scored, contactable pipeline — not raw leads.

4. **Conversion.** LeadScoreAI's follow-up team works the qualified pipeline through to a signed, funded loan, so the bank receives booked originations rather than a list to chase.

5. **Servicing feedback.** Repayment performance is fed back into the scoring engine, tightening accuracy on every subsequent cohort.

### Why the Willingness-to-Pay score matters to your risk team

The whole book lives or dies on selection. A model that reliably separates the borrowers who repay from those who do not is worth more to a lender than any volume of leads. LeadScoreAI's WTP score is designed to give your credit committee a defensible, improving signal at the exact moment of decision — reducing default rates and shrinking the cost of collections.

### The asset itself lowers the risk further

An inverter-and-battery system is a **discrete, identifiable asset.** It can be tied to the loan, insured, and — in the terms — recovered on serious default. Bundled asset insurance protects the collateral and adds a second revenue line. Combined with the WTP score, [BANK NAME] is lending against both a scored borrower *and* a secured asset.

### Built for volume, not for one loan at a time

LeadScoreAI's platform already handles bulk applicant intake, scoring and outcome tracking. As the book grows into thousands of loans, the qualification layer scales without adding proportional cost — the economics improve as the portfolio grows.

## PAGE 3 — Structure, commercials and next steps

### A phased partnership that de-risks the bank first

We propose starting with a **90-day pilot** before any long-term commitment, so [BANK NAME] proves the numbers on a small, controlled book before scaling.

**Phase 1 — Pilot (Months 1–3)**
- LeadScoreAI stands up the branded scorecard, campaigns and follow-up.
- A capped disbursement pool agreed with the bank (e.g. a set number of funded systems).
- Target outcomes measured and reported: qualified-lead volume, conversion-to-funded rate, and early repayment behaviour by WTP band.

**Phase 2 — Scale (Month 4 onward)**
- On proven metrics, the pool is expanded and the commercial model converts to per-funded-loan origination.
- Additional distributor partners and adjacent asset categories added under the same engine.

### Commercial model

LeadScoreAI is paid for outcomes, aligning our incentive directly with the quality of the book:

| Line | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Setup / integration** | One-time fee to stand up the scorecard, campaigns and scoring |
| **Origination fee** | A percentage of each funded loan's principal (structure agreed at pilot) |
| **Monitoring fee** | An ongoing per-loan fee for WTP and repayment monitoring over the loan life |
| **Insurance commission** | Shared commission on the bundled asset insurance |

We deliberately do **not** take an interest-spread share — [BANK NAME] remains unambiguously the lender, and LeadScoreAI remains the qualification and origination layer. Clean roles, clean regulation.
